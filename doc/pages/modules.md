Modules {#modules_page}
=======

SCAMPER SFE is made of five libraries and two programs. Each of them has its own Doxygen manual (see \ref build_docs); their
detailed descriptions are collected here.

Libraries:

- \subpage gmmlite_page — minimal Gaussian Mixture Models used as noise models.
- \subpage threadpool_page — fixed-size thread pool used to enroll and verify in parallel.
- \subpage turbocodes_page — soft-decoded Turbo Codes, PSK and 2-PAM modulations, file readers.
- \subpage hstcfe_page — the unimodal Hybrid Strengthened Turbo-Code Fuzzy Extractor.
- \subpage mhstcfe_page — the multimodal (feature-level fusion) extractor.

Programs:

- \subpage unimodal_program_page — unimodal enrollment and evaluation.
- \subpage multimodal_program_page — multimodal enrollment and evaluation with chimeric subjects.
